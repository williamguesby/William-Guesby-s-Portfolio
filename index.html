01 / THE PERSON

A Little
About Me.

I’m William, an Oklahoma State University student and aspiring developer. If it involves technology, chances are I’m interested!

What excites me about development is having an idea and figuring out how to make it real. I want to create things that feel like me, inspire someone else to try something new, and leave my own mark on the tech community along the way.

Away from the computer, you’ll probably find me on the basketball court, watching a game, or listening to music. And if I’m still at a screen, there’s a good chance I’m getting into an adventure game!
